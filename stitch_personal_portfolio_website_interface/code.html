[ 01 / Monograph ] Evolution & Architectural Thesis

From deterministic systems to the human substrate.

"Software is not decoration overlaid on code, but an interconnected system of state machines rendered visible. I prioritize measured reading cadences over ambient novelty, strict typographic hierarchy over decorative excess, and sub-second velocity over ornamental friction."

I began my career designing distributed microservices and database schemas. That background forever altered my perspective on interface architecture: every button click is a mutation, every screen transition a state transition, and every delay a breach of user trust.
